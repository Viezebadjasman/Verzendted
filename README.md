# Verzented

A small web app for selling clothes on Vinted: photograph an item, copy the listing text, keep the buyer's details, save the shipping QR code and the track & trace.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the app's link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Verzented from the home screen. It runs full screen and works offline.

## Good to know

- Everything is stored on the phone itself. Nothing is sent to a server.
- Each phone has its own rack; items are not shared between phones.
- Use **Save backup** at the bottom of the app now and then. Deleting the app from the home screen also deletes its data.
- Reading the tracking number from a photo happens on the phone. The first time it downloads the text reader (about 10 MB), so do that once on Wi-Fi.
